What We Do

Billing at the core, support across the whole practice

Claims, denials, and the full revenue cycle, plus practice management, revenue consulting, and healthcare technology guidance for practices that want one partner for the business side of medicine.

Claims Submission & Scrubbing

Clean claims go out fast and get paid the first time. We scrub every claim for coding errors, missing modifiers, and payer-specific rules before submission.

Denial Management & Appeals

Denials get worked, not written off. Our team tracks every denial to its root cause, files timely appeals, and feeds the fixes back into your front-end process.

Revenue Cycle Management

Full-cycle coverage: charge entry, payment posting, A/R follow-up, and monthly reporting that shows you exactly where every dollar stands.

Practice Management

Front- and back-office operations that run like clockwork. We help with staffing workflows, scheduling, and day-to-day process design so your office keeps pace with your revenue cycle.

Revenue Consulting

Performance analysis, payer contract strategy, and growth planning. We benchmark your numbers against your specialty and build a practical plan to capture more of what you earn.

Healthcare Technology Consulting

EHR selection and optimization, systems integration, and workflow automation. We help you choose, implement, and get real value from the technology your practice runs on.

Compliance & Security

Your patients' data, protected like it matters

Every workflow at Kalmed Billing is built around HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules. Protected health information is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based and audited, and every team member completes annual HIPAA and compliance training.

We sign a Business Associate Agreement with every client, maintain documented breach-response procedures, and run regular internal security reviews. Your compliance officer can audit us any time.

About Us

A billing partner, not a billing vendor

Kalmed Billing was founded by healthcare operations professionals who spent years inside medical practices and saw the same story everywhere: excellent clinicians losing hard-earned revenue to claim denials, coding errors, and understaffed billing desks.

We built Kalmed to fix that. Our team combines certified coders, payer-specialist claim analysts, and credentialing experts who treat your revenue cycle like it is their own. We keep our client roster deliberately focused so every practice gets senior attention.

And because billing never happens in isolation, we have built around it: practice management, revenue consulting, and healthcare technology consulting, so the whole business side of your practice has one accountable team.

Whether you are a solo practitioner or a multi-location group, we scale a billing operation to fit, and we are on the phone when you need us.
